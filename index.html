Inquiry & Reflection • IB PYP / MYP Criteria

Socratic Debrief & Assessment Matrix

Guide students to deconstruct their experience through inquiry questions on cultural language, geography, and how individual diversity strengthens the unified whole.

Inquiry 01 • Non-Verbal Language

Dance as Cultural Language

Despite speaking different regional languages across India, how did hand gestures (Mudras) and rhythms allow us to communicate and understand each other instantly without speaking?

Inquiry 02 • Physical Geography

Environmental Connections

Look at Bihu (harvest), Cheraw (bamboo), and Chhau (martial/nature). How do physical geography and local landscapes shape the movement style of each region?

Inquiry 03 • Synthesis Principle

Deconstructing "Many in the One"

When all regional traditions and state resources were synthesized in Phase 3, did individual cultures lose their unique identity, or did their uniqueness make the unified Indian tapestry richer?

Inquiry 04 • Real-World Transfer

Modern Societal Application

How can our classroom apply this "Cultural Matrix principle" when working in diverse project teams with people of different languages, talents, and backgrounds?

🌸 Navarasa: The 9 Classical Expressions

Natyashastra Abhinaya & Emotional Resonance Matrix
Authentic Natyashastra 9-Emotion Abhinaya System ("The Essence of Life")
1. Shringara (Love): The essence of attraction, beauty and affection.
2. Hasya (Joy): The expression of happiness, laughter and delight.
3. Karuna (Compassion): The feeling of empathy, kindness and sorrow for others.
4. Raudra (Anger): The power of anger that arises from injustice or ego.
5. Veera (Courage): The spirit of bravery, confidence and inner strength.
6. Bhayanaka (Fear): The emotion of fear, anxiety and apprehension.
7. Bibhatsa (Disgust): The feeling of aversion or repulsion towards something unpleasant.
8. Adbhuta (Wonder): The sense of awe, surprise and astonishment.
9. Shanta (Peace): The state of calmness, contentment and inner peace.
